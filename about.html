About Vertus

Our story

Designed for a calm stay

Vertus Hotel and Suites is a modern boutique hotel in Oka, Benin City — white and charcoal on the outside, warm wood and teal accents inside. The reception sets the tone: a sculpted wooden desk, soft light, and a quiet sense that someone has thought about the details.

Guests stay in bright rooms and suites, eat at ROKA, linger at the bar, and cool off in the private pool courtyard. LICIA by Vertus — Ivory Suites, Walnut and Amber — sits just beside the building. You’ll find us at 28 Akhionbare Street.

When you’re ready, send an enquiry with your dates, or call +234 912 888 8820. Check-in is after 4:00 PM; check-out before 1:00 PM.
